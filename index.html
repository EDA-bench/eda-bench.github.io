Overview

Across 35 PCB reconstruction tasks, a GPT-5.5 agent produced parseable KiCad projects for every board yet received a difficulty-weighted score of 0.92%. EDA-bench tests the electrical requirements those projects failed to reproduce: connector pin roles, routed signal paths and the expected responses at external interfaces.

The tasks are from open-hardware projects available to the public. Agents receive a hardware specification and a KiCad environment with web access; reference projects stay on the grader side. The evaluator matches connector pins, follows routed copper, applies deterministic checks and ngspice simulations where applicable, and checks KiCad electrical and design rules.

Scorer validation

Eight rotated and translated reference layouts retained full credit under the revised grader. The paper reports these representation biases alongside fault tests for pin swaps, shorts, missing routes and removed components.
