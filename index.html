Benchmark

A PCB may open in KiCad, but not provide the correct signals to a connector. EDA-bench measures the ability of language-model agents to recreate full KiCad projects that provide the intended electrical functionality at their external interfaces. Our benchmark consists of 35 tasks that were extracted from publicly available open-hardware projects. The examples range from simple connector breakout boards to power management circuits, motor drivers and compute carrier boards.

The grading system checks external pin matching and routed copper, applies deterministic tests, runs ngspice where applicable, and checks KiCad's electrical and design rules. Projects that can be parsed by the grading system are logged separately from those that satisfy the electrical specifications of the task. The evaluated systems have access to the web, and the tasks are therefore focused on reconstructing open-source projects.

Caveats

The paper describes fault tests, representation-related false rejections and limitations of the electrical models. These automated tests should not be viewed as indicators that a design is manufacturable, meets regulatory or electromagnetic compatibility requirements. The results may depend on the revision of the grading system and the policy for the source access that has been set.
